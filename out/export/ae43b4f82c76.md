1 Abstract
We want a tool developed to support efficient use of data storage, enabling us to
clean out large files but still retain the information at a minimum cost.
Addressing two issues:
 - Storage reduction — replace content as e.g video with text + key frames and
duplicates identification.
 - Data activation — make content searchable and AI-consumable.
The solution can thus include distilling data / compressing files and identifying
duplicate information across systems.
In addition, when an individual leaves the company, their files should be
analyzed, compared to existing copies, and deleted only if a duplicate already
exists.

2 Background and needs behind the initiative
Today, Ericsson stores and produces a large quantity of presentations, screen
recordings and videos from internal meetings, courses etc.
Each recording can be up to multiple hours long and often many hundreds of
megabytes large. Recordings like these consume digital storage space on any
platform that they are present on; SharePoint sites, One Drives or the third party
platform “Ericsson Play”.
As a result of the large size of the files this often leads to that highly informative
content often being deleted due to a lack of storage capacity.
Upon visiting internal sites, documents are often also downloaded to laptop and
in turn again upload into One-drives of different users leading to duplications.
Also as regulations were enforced, site administrators and owners had to start
deleting content from their SharePoint sites when the maximum storage capacity
was reached. Permanently deleting content is a problem since all information
within the files get lost, especially in a time of digitalization and AI where data is
considered the new gold.
Deleting files loses the opportunity to use the data as training data for future AI
models. If it would be possible to minimize the data storage need for recordings
etc., we could store more of them for a longer time, enable access to them by
employees and use the information as training data for AI agents and models.
.
3 Basic idea
Processing recordings using a new tool could transform video content into other
forms of data, such as text files and single image frames of e.g. a powerpoint
slide..
When processing videos with the tool, information within the videos gets turned
into text format. Data in the form of text is easier to digest by AI models.
By transforming certain types of videos into clean text data, future internal AI
agents will grow stronger and current internal AI models, can benefit from new
available data. AI technologies already contribute to a more efficient way of
working, and by improving AI models this efficiency will further increase.
Video data requires more storage space compared to other forms of data such as
text, image and audio. Through compressing meeting recordings into chunks of
smaller forms of data the tool will decrease the current costs related to digital
storage and increase efficiency when sharing and uploading recordings. We
estimate that a realistic scenario would be that 2-hour recording (~1 GB)
becomes a ~50 KB of text plus ~2 MB of slide frames — roughly a 99% reduction
— while keeping the information that matters.
